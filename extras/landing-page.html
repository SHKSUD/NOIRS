The pipeline

From one line to a moving storyboard

Every step is editable. Change a shot, redraw a panel, or rewrite the scene and run it again.

IDEA

Write the scene

One sentence becomes a formatted screenplay in English, Hindi or Hinglish.

BREAKDOWN

Plan the shots

Shot type, lens, camera move, mood and duration for every beat.

BOARD

Sketch the panels

Pencil-and-ink frames with motion arrows and consistent characters.

SCORE

Check the style

A 0–10 match against the director's techniques, with what's missing.

ANIMATIC

Play it back

Camera moves, subtitles and voice. Export as video or PDF.
